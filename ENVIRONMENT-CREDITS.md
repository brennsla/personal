# Environment materials

Downloaded from ambientCG, licensed CC0:

- Concrete034: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Concrete034 — concrete/plaster PBR material, source technique: approximation.
- Ground037: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Ground037 — photogrammetry ground surface.

Color, OpenGL normal, and roughness maps resized from 1K to 512px and JPEG-encoded for the offline game. Embedded in src/assets/environment/maps.js; rebuild helper: scripts/pack-environment-maps.mjs (uses the local bundled sharp runtime).

Cloud sprites and articulated pedestrians are procedural, not photographic assets or scanned characters.

These permissions cover these environment maps only, not the separately sourced vehicle models.
